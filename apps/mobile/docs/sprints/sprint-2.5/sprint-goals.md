# Sprint 2.5 Goals: Medication Intelligence & Notification Platform

## Theme
From storing reminders to ensuring people actually take their medicines.

## Goal
Build an enterprise-grade, offline-first medication execution engine that delivers reminders reliably, tracks adherence, and supports family visibility.

## Key Objectives
1. **Production-Ready Notifications**: Ensure local notifications are scheduled securely, support timezone nuances, and recover gracefully after device reboots, working 100% offline.
2. **Actionable Adherence Metrics**: Provide intelligent metrics (daily, weekly, monthly, streaks) categorizing adherence into risk levels.
3. **Family Coordination**: Share medication adherence states across the family structure so caregivers have visibility into missed or taken doses.
4. **Rich Analytics Dashboard**: Enhance the main dashboard with at-a-glance analytics, alerting, and streak progression.
