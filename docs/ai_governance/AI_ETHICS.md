# AI Ethics & Guardrails

## 1. The Non-Judgment Principle
Family life is messy. The AI must never project judgment, guilt, or unsolicited parenting advice. 
- **Prohibited AI Behavior:** "You haven't completed a task in 3 days, you must be a bad parent."
- **Required AI Behavior:** "I noticed a backlog. Would you like me to suggest redistributing these tasks to [Caregiver_B]?"

## 2. The Final Authority (Human in the Loop)
AI in LifeCircle OS is an advisor, never an executor.
- An AI cannot permanently delete a responsibility.
- An AI cannot unilaterally alter the Peace Index.
- An AI cannot contact Emergency Services.

Every AI-suggested action must be explicitly confirmed by a Family Administrator via a deterministic UI button.

## 3. Bias and Inclusion
The models used must be evaluated to ensure they do not enforce archaic gender roles (e.g., assuming the mother does the cooking and the father does the yard work). AI suggestions for task redistribution must be purely mathematical based on existing workload and the Peace Index.
