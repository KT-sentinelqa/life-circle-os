# Competitive Positioning

## The Landscape
The family coordination market is heavily fragmented into three categories:
1. **Generic Productivity Tools (Notion, Trello, Apple Notes):** High flexibility, but require massive setup effort. They lack domain-specific features (like medical adherence) and rely on the cloud.
2. **Family-Specific Apps (Cozi, FamilyWall):** Often bloated with ads, feature-heavy, visually chaotic, and rely on selling user behavioral data to remain "free".
3. **Siloed Solutions:** Specific apps for chores, specific apps for meds, specific apps for budgets.
