# Long Term Strategy (3-Year Horizon)

## Phase 1: Establish Trust (Year 1)
- **Objective:** Become the single source of truth for the daily operational load of a nuclear family.
- **Key Results:** 
  - 10,000 active families.
  - 99.9% crash-free rate.
  - 0 major security or privacy breaches.
- **Focus Areas:** The Peace Index, Responsibility Engine, and Emergency Module.

## Phase 2: Expand the Circle (Year 2)
- **Objective:** Incorporate extended family members (grandparents, external caregivers) safely through strict RBAC.
- **Key Results:**
  - 50,000 active families.
  - Launch of "Caregiver" and "Observer" roles.
  - Introduction of the Medical Adherence & Prescription tracking module.
- **Focus Areas:** Asymmetric UI (simple views for elderly, detailed views for administrators), cross-household sync.

## Phase 3: The Family AI Assistant (Year 3)
- **Objective:** Deploy localized, privacy-preserving AI to proactively manage logistics.
- **Key Results:**
  - 250,000 active families.
  - 30% reduction in manual "Add Responsibility" actions due to AI anticipation.
- **Focus Areas:** On-device ML models (Apple Neural Engine / Android AICore) that analyze routine and suggest task delegation without ever transmitting raw behavioral data to the cloud.
