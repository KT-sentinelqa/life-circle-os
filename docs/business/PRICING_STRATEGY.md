# Pricing Strategy

## The Philosophy
If you are not paying for the product, you are the product. LifeCircle OS protects family privacy above all else. Therefore, we **do not** offer an ad-supported free tier. We monetize strictly through direct software subscriptions.

## The Model: Flat-Rate Household Subscription

**Price:** $99 / Year (or $9.99 / Month) per Household.

### Included in the Subscription:
- Unlimited Family Members (parents, children, grandparents).
- Unlimited Devices.
- End-to-End Encrypted Cloud Sync.
- All Core Modules (Responsibilities, Emergency, Medical).

### Why Flat-Rate?
Per-seat pricing penalizes families for adding caregivers or grandparents. A flat household rate encourages the inclusion of the entire family unit, which directly improves the product's effectiveness and stickiness.

## Free Trial
- 30-Day unrestricted Free Trial. No credit card required upfront to reduce friction and allow the Peace Index to demonstrate value.
