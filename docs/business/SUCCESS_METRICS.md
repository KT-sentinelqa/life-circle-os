# Success Metrics

We do not measure success by Time-in-App. In fact, if Time-in-App goes down while task completion stays stable, the product is succeeding (we are reducing friction).

## Primary KPIs (North Star)
1. **Household Retention (Month 3):** The percentage of households that convert to a paid subscription after the trial and remain active past month 3. Target: >60%.
2. **Peace Index Stability:** The aggregate rolling average of family Peace Scores. A rising Peace Score indicates we are successfully reducing mental load.

## Secondary KPIs (Operational)
1. **Invite Acceptance Rate:** Percentage of generated invites that are successfully claimed by a secondary family member. Target: >80%.
2. **Crash-Free Sessions:** Target: >99.9%.
3. **Responsibility Completion Rate:** Percentage of assigned responsibilities checked off within 24 hours of their deadline.

## Anti-Metrics (What we actively minimize)
1. **Time-in-App per Session:** We want users in and out in under 15 seconds.
2. **Push Notification Volume:** We aim to reduce the number of nudges required to complete a task.
