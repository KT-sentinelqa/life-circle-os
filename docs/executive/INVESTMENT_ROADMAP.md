# Investment & Capital Roadmap

## Pre-Seed (Current Stage)
- **Status:** Bootstrapped / Founder Funded.
- **Runway:** Optimized by utilizing local device compute (Offline-first) to minimize cloud AWS/GCP bills.
- **Goal:** Reach General Availability and 100 paying households ($10k ARR) to prove willingness to pay.

## Seed Round (Horizon: Post-GA)
- **Target Raise:** $1.5M - $2M.
- **Use of Funds:**
  1. **Security & Compliance:** Formal SOC2 Type II certification and third-party penetration testing to cement trust.
  2. **Go-To-Market:** Content marketing targeting the "Mental Load" and privacy-conscious parental demographics.
  3. **Engineering:** Hire 2 Senior Flutter/Dart engineers to accelerate the Medical Adherence module.
- **Milestone Required to Raise:** 1,000 active, paying households ($100k ARR) and a proven >60% retention rate at month 3.

## Series A (Horizon: Year 2)
- **Target Raise:** $5M - $8M.
- **Use of Funds:** Expansion into the Elder Care and Co-parenting (Divorce coordination) markets. Scaling the localized AI features.
- **Milestone Required to Raise:** $1M - $1.5M ARR with high capital efficiency (LTV:CAC > 5).
