# Quarterly Planning (Cadence)

LifeCircle OS operates on a strict 6-week operational cycle, loosely inspired by Basecamp's Shape Up methodology, adapted for an Enterprise-grade privacy startup.

## The Cycle (6 Weeks)
- **Weeks 1-4 (Execution):** Engineering and Product teams build the prioritized features. No context switching. No "urgent" interruptions unless a SEV-1 incident occurs.
- **Week 5 (Cooldown & Certification):** Feature freeze. Execution of the QA-004 Release Certification pipeline. Generation of Goldens. Penetration testing of new endpoints.
- **Week 6 (Planning):** The Executive Team reviews the Design Partner feedback, the Risk Register, and the OKRs. The next cycle is "shaped" and prioritized.

## The Rule of Three (Validation)
No feature enters the 6-Week Cycle unless three independent Design Partner families have explicitly validated the problem it solves. We do not build features based on internal assumptions.
