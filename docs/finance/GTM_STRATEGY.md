# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Target Audience
**The Primary Buyer (The "General Manager"):** Usually a mother or father aged 30-50, managing a dual-income household, with children under 18 and potentially aging parents. They feel the overwhelming burden of the "mental load" and are actively seeking a structural solution.

## 2. Customer Journey
- **Who buys?** The primary caregiver (General Manager).
- **Who invites?** The buyer invites their partner (The "Co-Manager"), children, and external caregivers/grandparents.
- **Who advocates?** Highly organized caregivers who experience a measurable drop in anxiety (validated via the Peace Index) will evangelize the product in parent groups.
- **Who churns?** Families who fail to build the habit of checking the Dashboard daily within the first 14 days.

## 3. Acquisition Channels
- **Organic Word of Mouth:** "It reduced our arguments by 50%."
- **Content Marketing:** Essays and manifestos on the "Mental Load" and the "Enterprise of the Family."
- **Privacy-focused Communities:** Tech-savvy parents seeking alternatives to data-hungry legacy apps.

## 4. Sales Motion (B2C Subscriptions)
- **Frictionless Trial:** 30 days free. No credit card upfront.
- **The "Aha!" Moment:** When a partner completes a responsibility without being asked, and the Peace Index visibly improves.
