# Children's Privacy Policy (COPPA/GDPR-K)

LifeCircle OS is designed to coordinate families, which inherently involves children. We have designed the system specifically to protect them.

## 1. We Do Not Target Children
The LifeCircle OS account must be created by a legally responsible adult (The Family Administrator). 

## 2. No Child PII Required
We do not ask for, nor do we require, a child's real name, date of birth, or email address to create a "Child Role" profile within the household. You may use nicknames, emojis, or aliases.

## 3. Closed Network
A child's device linked to LifeCircle OS cannot interact with anyone outside the cryptographically secure Household. There are no public profiles, no search directories, and no messaging with strangers.
