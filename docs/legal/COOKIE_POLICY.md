# Cookie & Local Storage Policy

LifeCircle OS is primarily a mobile application. However, any web portals used for account management or support adhere to this policy.

## 1. No Third-Party Trackers
We do not use Facebook Pixels, Google Analytics, or any third-party marketing cookies. 

## 2. Essential Local Storage
The LifeCircle OS mobile app relies heavily on local storage (the Isar Database) to function in Offline Mode. This is a functional requirement, not a tracking mechanism.
- We store Session Tokens securely in the device Keychain/Keystore (ADR-034).
- We store your encrypted family data locally so the app works without an internet connection.

By using the app, you consent to the storage of this essential operational data on your personal device.
