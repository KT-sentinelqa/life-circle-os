# Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Archetype
LifeCircle OS is **The Guardian / The Architect**. We provide structure, safety, and calm in a chaotic world.

## 2. Voice and Tone
- **Calm & Deterministic:** We do not use exclamation marks heavily. We do not use "Oops!" or "Uh oh!". We state facts clearly. (e.g., "Network unavailable. Changes saved locally." instead of "Oops! We can't reach the server right now!").
- **Professional but Empathetic:** We acknowledge that family logistics are hard without sounding patronizing.
- **Privacy-Forward:** We explicitly remind users that we cannot see their data.

## 3. Visual Identity
- **Primary Color:** `Escalation Rose` (Reserved exclusively for high-priority/emergency actions).
- **Base Colors:** Off-whites, slate grays, and muted earth tones to reduce visual stimulation.
- **Typography:** `Inter` (sans-serif) for high legibility across all generations and screen sizes.

## 4. Prohibited Marketing Tactics
- No fear-mongering (e.g., "Don't let your grandmother forget her pills!").
- No gamification marketing (e.g., "Level up your parenting!").
- No comparison to social networks.
