# LifeCircle OS - Design Partner Consent Form

**Version:** 1.0
**Classification:** Internal Confidential / Legally Binding
**Data Handling Policy:** SEC-017 (Telemetry) & SEC-025 (Backend Privacy)

## 1. Purpose of the Study
You are participating in the Design Partner Program for LifeCircle OS, a system designed to coordinate family responsibilities and reduce mental load. The goal of this session is to observe your natural behavior and gather your candid feedback.

## 2. What to Expect
- The session will last approximately 45-60 minutes.
- You will be asked to interact with a prototype or concept application.
- There are no right or wrong answers. We are testing the system, not you.

## 3. Data Collection & Privacy (Recording Policy)
- **Recordings:** With your explicit permission below, this session will be audio/video recorded solely for internal research purposes.
- **Storage:** Recordings are stored on encrypted, access-controlled drives. They will never be shared publicly or uploaded to third-party AI transcription services without explicit enterprise security agreements.
- **Deletion:** All raw recordings will be permanently deleted 90 days after the session date, in accordance with our Data Retention Policy. Synthesized (anonymized) notes will be retained indefinitely.
- **Anonymization:** Your name will not be attached to the feedback in our engineering systems. Your family will be referenced only by an anonymized ID (e.g., F001).

## 4. Voluntary Participation & Withdrawal
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question, request that the recording be stopped, or withdraw from the session entirely at any time without penalty or consequence.

If you have questions about this research or your privacy rights, please contact the Lead Researcher at [Email Address].
