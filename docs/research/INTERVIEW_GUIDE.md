# F001-F010 Interview Guide

## 1. Preparation
* Ensure `CONSENT_FORM.md` is signed.
* Start recording (if consented).
* **Interviewer Reminder:** Do not lead the witness. Embrace silence. Ask "Why?" at least three times.

## 2. Warm-up (5 mins)
* "Tell me a bit about how responsibilities are currently divided in your household."
* "When a shared task (like picking up medicine or paying a bill) is completed, how do you usually find out it was done?"

## 3. Observation / Prototype Interaction (20 mins)
* *Provide the prototype / app to the primary user.*
* "I’d like you to explore this for a few minutes. Please think out loud as you look at it. Tell me what you're seeing and what you think it means."
* **Task 1:** "Show me how you would check the status of your family's responsibilities today."
* **Task 2:** "Imagine you just picked up the prescription. Show me what you would do next."
* **Task 3:** "Look at the 'Peace Score' at the top. What does that number mean to you?"

## 4. Pain & Reaction Probing (15 mins)
* "You mentioned earlier that [insert pain point from Warm-up]. How does what you just saw compare to how you handle that today?"
* "If this system didn't send you a notification when your spouse finished a task, how would that make you feel?" (Testing Exception-Based Alerting)
* "What is the absolute worst part about managing household tasks right now?"

## 5. Closing (5 mins)
* "If you had a magic wand and could change one thing about how your family coordinates, what would it be?"
* "Is there anything else you think I should know?"
* *Thank the participant, stop recording, inform them of next steps.*
