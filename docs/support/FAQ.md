# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does LifeCircle OS work without the internet?
**Yes.** LifeCircle OS is built on an "Offline-First" architecture. You can create responsibilities, complete tasks, and update your Peace Index while completely disconnected (e.g., on an airplane or in a hospital basement). Once your device regains connectivity, it will securely sync changes with the rest of your family.

### 2. Can you see my family's data?
**No.** We employ a Zero Trust architecture. We do not sell your data, we do not analyze your text for advertising, and we actively scrub potential Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before telemetry reaches our servers.

### 3. How do I invite a grandparent or external caregiver?
Go to **Settings > Family Invite**. Generate a secure 8-character invite code and send it to them. They do not need to share their phone number or email with the platform to join your household.

### 4. Why is there no "Free" tier?
Ad-supported platforms survive by selling your behavioral data. We believe a family's operating system must be incorruptible. Our flat-rate subscription ensures you are our only customer, not advertisers.

### 5. What is the Peace Index?
The Peace Index is a real-time, privacy-preserving metric (from 0 to 100) indicating the overall operational health of your household. It factors in completed responsibilities, overdue items, and workload distribution to give you a quick glance at your family's stress levels.
