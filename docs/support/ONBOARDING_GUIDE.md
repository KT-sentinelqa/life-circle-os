# Onboarding Guide

**How to set up LifeCircle OS for your family.**

## Phase 1: The Administrator Setup
1. **Download the App:** Install LifeCircle OS on your primary device.
2. **Create a Household:** You will be prompted to create a secure Household. We will generate a cryptographic keypair on your device.
3. **Set your Passkey:** Secure your app using your device's biometric authentication (FaceID/TouchID) or a Passkey.

## Phase 2: Adding the Essentials
1. **Add your first Responsibility:** Go to the Dashboard and tap the `+` icon. Add something simple like "Take out the recycling."
2. **Setup Emergency Contacts:** Go to the Emergency tab and add your primary pediatrician or local urgent care number.

## Phase 3: Inviting the Family
1. Go to **Settings > Invite Family Member**.
2. Select the role (e.g., Parent, Caregiver).
3. Generate the 8-character code.
4. Have the other person download the app and enter the code during setup.
5. They are now securely synced with your household.

*You are now ready to operate calmly.*
