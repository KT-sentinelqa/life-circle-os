# LifeCircle OS: Design Partner Agreement

**Welcome to the LifeCircle Family!**

Thank you for agreeing to be an early Design Partner for LifeCircle OS. We are on a mission to build the definitive Operating System for Indian Families, and we cannot do it without the real-world wisdom of families like yours.

## 🤝 What We Ask of You
As a Design Partner, we ask for a small commitment of your time to help shape the future of this product:
1. **Three Feedback Sessions**: Over the next 4–6 weeks, we’d love to have three brief (30-minute) conversations with you to test new prototypes and ideas.
2. **Brutal Honesty**: If a feature is confusing, unnecessary, or annoying, tell us immediately. Your critical feedback prevents us from building the wrong things.
3. **Beta Testing**: When we launch our private beta, we ask that you attempt to use it with your family for at least one week.

## 🛡️ Our Promise to You
Family data is sacred. We treat it with the highest level of respect and security.
1. **Absolute Privacy**: Any data, stories, or insights you share during our sessions will be anonymized. Your family's specific financial or medical situations will never be shared publicly.
2. **No Data Mining**: LifeCircle OS is built on an offline-first architecture. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads.
3. **Direct Line to Founders**: You will have a direct channel to the core engineering and product team. If you need something fixed, you skip the line.

## 🎁 A Token of Our Gratitude
Building a company is a community effort. To thank you for your time and insights:
* **Lifetime Premium Access**: As a founding Design Partner, your family will receive a lifetime subscription to LifeCircle OS Premium once it launches.
* **Early Access to Features**: You will always be the first to experience and shape our newest tools.
