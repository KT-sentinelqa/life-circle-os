# LifeCircle OS: User Discovery Interview Script

**Objective**: Uncover the visceral pain points of family management, bypassing assumed solutions to discover actual behavior.
**Duration**: 30-45 minutes.

## 1. Introduction & Context (5 mins)
* "Thank you so much for taking the time."
* "We are building something to help families manage their daily chaos, but before we write any more code, we need to know how real families actually operate."
* "There are no right or wrong answers. If our app sounds useless to you, please tell us! That is the most valuable feedback we can get."

## 2. Current State Audit (15 mins)
*We want to understand their current 'tech stack' for family management.*

**Parent Care**
* "Who primarily manages the elders' medicines or doctor appointments in your house?"
* "How do you ensure they don't forget? (e.g., WhatsApp, phone calls, memory?)"
* "What is the most stressful part of managing their health from afar or alongside your own work?"

**Finances & Bills**
* "How do you track EMIs, school fees, and electricity bills?"
* "Has a bill ever slipped through the cracks? What happened?"
* "How many reminders do you actually want before a bill is due?"

**Household Responsibilities**
* "Who is the default 'coordinator' of the house?"
* "When duties (like groceries or renewals) get delegated, how is that tracked?"
* "What household responsibility causes the most friction or arguments?"

## 3. The "Magic Wand" Question (5 mins)
* "If I gave you a magic wand and you could instantly permanently offload ONE family responsibility from your brain forever, what would it be?"

## 4. Concept Testing & Demo (10 mins)
* *Show the LifeCircle OS Showcase (v0.3.1-showcase-final).*
* *Walk through the Sharma Family scenario (Medicines, Timeline, EMIs, Duties).*
* **Watch their face**: Note moments of confusion, relief, or delight.
* "What is your immediate reaction to this?"
* "Which of these cards (Medicines, EMI, Insurance, Duties) feels the most urgent to you?"

## 5. Value Validation (5 mins)
* "Is this something you would install for your family today?"
* "If this successfully took [Magic Wand Problem] off your plate, how much would you pay for it per month?"
* "What is the ONE thing missing here that would make this indispensable for your household?"

## Closing
* "Thank you. Would you be open to being a Design Partner as we build this out?"
